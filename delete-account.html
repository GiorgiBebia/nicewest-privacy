Privacy Policy / კონფიდენციალურობის პოლიტიკა

English

Option 1: inside the app (fastest)

  1. Open the NiceWest app and sign in.
  2. Go to Settings.
  3. Tap Delete account and confirm.

Option 2: by email (if you cannot open the app)

Send an email to nicewestsupport@gmail.com with the subject Delete my NiceWest account from the email address you registered with, and include your username. We will verify the request and delete the account within 7 days.

Send deletion request

What is deleted

  • Immediately removed: your profile, photos, likes, matches, messages, blocks and reports, device records and sign-in sessions.
  • Kept for up to 90 days in restricted storage, then permanently deleted automatically: email address, username, device identifier, push token, registration IP address, and copies of deleted messages and photos. This is used only to prevent fraud and repeated abuse and to meet legal obligations.
  • After deleting, you can register again after the waiting period shown in the app.
