1. Who we are

NiceWest is a dating and social-discovery mobile app for adults (18+). This document explains what personal data the app collects, why, who it is shared with, how long it is kept, and how you can control it. Contact: nicewestsupport@gmail.com.

2. Age restriction (18+)

NiceWest is strictly for people who are at least 18 years old. By registering you confirm that you are 18 or older. Accounts of minors are prohibited and will be removed.

3. Data we collect

Data Why
Account data: username, email address, password (stored only as a one-way hash), sign-in method (email or Google) Create and secure your account, send verification codes
Profile data: full name, date of birth / age, gender, who you are looking for, city, bio, interests, preferences (age range, search radius), language Show your profile and find suitable matches
Photos you upload Display on your profile; reviewed by our moderators
Location: approximate and precise GPS coordinates, only while you use the app and only with your permission Show people nearby and distances; apply your search radius
Activity: likes, dislikes, matches, messages you send and receive, blocks, reports Provide matching and chat, keep the community safe
Device and technical data: device brand/model, OS version, device identifier, push notification token, IP address, app-open events Send notifications, prevent fraud and repeated abuse, keep the service secure
Advertising data: advertising identifier and ad interaction data processed by Google AdMob Show ads that fund the app (we request non-personalised ads)

We do not collect your contacts, microphone recordings, or your camera feed. Photos are chosen by you from your gallery.

4. Profile review

New and changed profiles (photos, name, bio, etc.) are reviewed by NiceWest administrators before they become visible to other users. Administrators can see the data you submit for review.

5. Who can see your data

  • Other users: your approved profile (photos, name, age, city, bio, interests, approximate distance) is visible to other users of the app according to matching settings. Messages are visible only to you and the person you chat with, and to administrators if a message or user is reported.
  • Service providers (processors) that help us run the app: Supabase (database and photo storage), Render (server hosting), Brevo (sending verification and password-reset emails), Google (Google Sign-In, Firebase Cloud Messaging, Google AdMob), Expo (push notification delivery and app updates).
  • Authorities: if required by law or to protect safety and rights.

We do not sell your personal data.

6. How long we keep data

  • While your account is active we keep your profile, photos, matches and messages.
  • When you delete your account, your profile, photos, likes, matches, messages, blocks and device records are removed from the live service immediately.
  • To prevent fraud and repeated abuse and to comply with legal obligations, a limited archive (email address, username, device identifier, push token, registration IP address, and copies of deleted messages and photos) is kept in restricted storage for up to 90 days and then permanently deleted automatically.

7. Your choices and rights

  • Edit your profile and photos in Settings.
  • Turn off location access in your phone settings at any time (some features will then be limited).
  • Turn off notifications in your phone settings.
  • Block or report any user from their profile or chat.
  • Delete your account inside the app (Settings → Delete account) or via our account deletion page.
  • Ask for a copy of your data or a correction by emailing nicewestsupport@gmail.com.

8. Security

Data is transmitted over encrypted connections (HTTPS). Passwords are stored only as salted hashes. Access to administration tools is limited to authorised administrators. No system is perfectly secure, so please use a strong, unique password.

9. Community guidelines (user-generated content)

Be respectful. Harassment, hate speech, nudity or adult content, non-consensual intimate content, spam, fraud, impersonation and fake profiles are prohibited and lead to removal of content and a ban of the account. Users can report and block others in the app; reports are reviewed by administrators.

10. Safety and limitation of liability

NiceWest does not conduct criminal background checks and is not responsible for interactions or meetings between users, online or offline. Please be careful when sharing personal information and meet in public places first.

11. Changes

We may update this document. The date at the top shows the latest version. Continuing to use the app after a change means you accept it.
